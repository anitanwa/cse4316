This section defines the project organization --- the stakeholders, points of contact, and the team's internal structure --- corresponding to the project organization content of ISO/IEC/IEEE 16326:2019 \cite{ISO16326}. Who are the stakeholders of the project? Who will be the point of contact from the sponsor or customer side? Who are the team members, and what will be their areas of responsibility? Will your team maintain the same Product Owner and Scrum Master for the whole project, or will those roles change periodically? A responsibility-assignment table (team member vs. area of responsibility) is recommended. This section should occupy 1/2 - 1 full page.
